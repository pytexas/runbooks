# Swag Runbook

Swag ordering is owned by the Venue Committee.
Marketing runs the campaigns around it, including the shirt pre-order last-chance push.

See `marketing-committee-runbook.md` and `venue-committee-runbook.md`.

## What Swag Is

- Shirts: a paid pre-order add-on sold alongside tickets on Pretix, with free-shirt comp classes (speakers, organizers, sponsor allotments).
- Stickers, patches, and enamel pins: the standing small-item line. 2026 added a 20th-anniversary line (patches, pins, rubber ducks).
- Speaker gifts: one nicer item per year.
- Badges and lanyards: separate budget lines; the lanyard is usually sponsor-funded.

A shirt does not come with a ticket.
Say so plainly in ticket copy and the KBYG; attendee confusion about this recurs every year.

Plan quantities against real capacity: 250 is the sell-out number, stretchable to 275.

### The Universal-Shirt Question

Giving every attendee a shirt was debated in the 2026 cycle and not adopted; shirts stayed a paid pre-order.
The 2026 post-event survey asked whether attendees would want a free shirt included next year, and most said yes.
Treat this as an open question for each year's budget discussion, not as current practice.

## Order Sequencing (2026 Pattern)

1. ~Week -11: ideation docs (shirt ideas, other swag ideas, speaker gift candidates) with supplier links and minimum-order tradeoffs. Commission the shirt design.
2. ~Week -8: gather vendor quotes. Remind vendors the Foundation's 501(c)(3) status removes sales tax; the 2026 quote dropped once the vendor applied it.
3. ~Week -6.5: pay the first order (patches, stickers, pins).
4. ~Week -5: shirt pre-orders close. Marketing runs a last-chance campaign the week before.
5. ~Week -4.5: place the shirt order immediately after pre-orders close.
6. ~Week -2.5: everything ordered and approved.

Hold sponsor-funded items (lanyards) until the sponsor has paid.
The whole window is Weeks -11 to -2.5; no swag is ordered in December.

## Vendors

- Kong Screenprinting (Austin) for shirts and screen printing; quotes come through Printavo. Rocksports was the 2023-2024 vendor.
- Do not let the vendor alter the logo. The 2026 print vendor modified it and degraded it; require the supplied art as-is unless there is a real production reason.
- Stickers: the well-known marketplace vendor upcharges; use alternatives (Stickerbeat in 2024).
- Badges: ConferenceBadges.com (template, print ahead plus day-of). The badge order slipped to the final two weeks in both 2025 and 2026; budget it earlier.
- Specialty items: re-quote before committing; 2026 found a cheaper rubber duck vendor by asking again.

## Shirts

- Sold as a paid pre-order add-on on Pretix; sizes are collected at purchase for pre-orders, but comp classes, day-of sales, and buffer stock still need a deliberate size estimate. 2026 ran short on day 1.
- Comp classes are tracked in the budget sheet.
- Staff shirts: yellow for general organizers, tie-dye for the program/speaker team. Collect staff sizes early.
- Give sponsors a pre-order deadline (~5 weeks out) for guaranteed day-of pickup.
- Distribution is by poker chip redemption at a staffed shirt table. Chips need pre-sorting or stuffing into badges ahead of time, and that job cannot fall on one person during registration.
- When sizes run short, stand up a T-shirt waitlist form (2026 did this on day 1).
- Hand shirts out in paper bags.

## Speaker Gifts

- 2026: embroidered BBQ aprons
- 2025: cups
- 2024: etched map glasses (Well Told Design)

## Day-Of Swag Sales

- Extra swag (anniversary items, prior-year shirts at a discount) sells day-of through Pretix voucher-redemption QR codes, not cash.
- Generate the QR codes with segno (Python library); publish them in docs posted at the swag table.
- Set the swag store up ahead of time, not during registration.

## Lessons Learned

- 2026: the print vendor altered the logo and degraded it; lock the art.
- 2026: shirt sizes ran short on day 1; the waitlist form saved it, but choose buffer sizes deliberately.
- 2026: 501(c)(3) status removes sales tax; say so when requesting quotes.
- 2025 and 2026: badges slipped to the final two weeks both years; order earlier.
- 2025: poker chips work but need a pre-sorting system and a staffed table; do not depend on one person stuffing badges.
- Recurring: attendees are confused about whether a shirt comes with the ticket; state it everywhere tickets are sold.
