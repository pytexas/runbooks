# Finance Committee Runbook

The Finance Committee owns the budget sheet, sponsorship end to end, registration money and reporting, opportunity grants, the PSF grant, insurance, taxes, and the Foundation's bank accounts.

See `budget-planning-template.md`, `sponsorship.md`, `registration.md`, `grants.md`.

## Standing Practices

- One budget sheet per year, owned by the finance lead. Anyone can look; nobody edits without talking to the owner. See `budget-planning-template.md`.
- Two bank accounts (Grasshopper Bank and a credit union account). Balances are read aloud at meetings; they never go in shared docs or public pages.
- Weekly Pretix accounting reports, exported to PDF every Monday from about 14 weeks out through conference week and filed in the year's Finance Reports folder in Drive.
- Registration stats reported at every planning meeting once sales open: counts per ticket class, shirt pre-orders, revenue against target.
- The Foundation is a 501(c)(3), which removes sales tax on vendor orders. Remind every vendor; a 2026 swag quote dropped once the vendor applied the exemption.
- Sequence dependent purchases behind the sponsor payment that funds them. 2026 held the lanyard order until the lanyard sponsor paid.
- Keep every hard payment date in the standing Upcoming Deadlines block of the minutes.

## Month -12: New Budget

- [ ] Create next year's budget sheet during the current conference weekend (the 2026 sheet was created on day 1 of the 2025 event)
- [ ] Carry forward last year's structure; review actuals and variance per line
- [ ] Draft targets with the Conference Chair: revenue goal, expense ceiling, profit target

See `next-year-planning.md` for the parallel venue and dates workstream.

## Month -8: Kickoff

- [ ] Present the budget at kickoff and settle the year's financial goals
- [ ] Review sponsorship tiers against last year's sales and retro notes
- [ ] Confirm ticket classes and rough pricing direction

## Month -7: Prospectus

- [ ] Finalize the sponsor prospectus for publication by early October
- [ ] Publish it and email it to the sponsor mailing list (the per-year Google Group of sponsor contacts), coordinating announcements with Marketing
- [ ] Build the sponsor tracking sheet and start outreach, prior sponsors first

Corporate budget season is the reason for the early-October deadline; slipping past October loses sponsors whose budgets close.
Know each prospect's fiscal-year end: a 2024 Gold sponsorship closed only because that company's budget expired January 31.

## Month -5.5 to -4: Contracts and Ticket Setup

- [ ] Clone per-tier sponsor agreement templates from last year
- [ ] Set the ticket timeline with Venue: early bird opens December 1 and closes mid-January
- [ ] Prepare the sponsor invoice template (mid-December)
- [ ] Open the opportunity grant application about 4 months out (late December); see `grants.md`

## Month -4.5 to -3: Sales Open, Grant Season, PSF

- [ ] Early bird tickets open on Pretix December 1
- [ ] Apply for the PSF grant about 3 months out
- [ ] Pay the venue balance (due about 3 months out)
- [ ] Pay the A/V vendor deposit
- [ ] Start the weekly Pretix accounting reports (about 14 weeks out)

The PSF grant requires a published schedule, links to the Code of Conduct and policies, and a budget, and processing takes about 8 weeks.
Apply earlier than feels necessary.
The PSF froze grants without warning in February 2025, and the freeze plus the 8-week lead forced a scramble; external freezes can happen again.

## Week -12 to -8: Early Bird Close and Grant Review

- [ ] Early bird ends around January 19; coordinate the countdown campaign with Marketing
- [ ] Chase unsigned sponsor contracts and unpaid invoices
- [ ] Opportunity grant applications close about 8.5 weeks out; review them in a dedicated special meeting the next day, not the regular planning meeting (see `grants.md`)
- [ ] Confirm catering and dinner-venue deposits with Venue (deposits run about half of each total)

## Week -8 to -4: Insurance and Fulfillment

- [ ] Buy event insurance through The Event Helper. Hard deadline about 4.5 weeks out.
- [ ] Fulfill grants: send emails, send funds, comp tickets, and track each to completion
- [ ] Track sponsor comp-ticket redemption; two sponsors never redeemed theirs in 2025
- [ ] Pay swag, catering, and linens invoices as Venue places orders (orders run Weeks -8 to -2.5)

2026 met the insurance deadline only because it headlined the Upcoming Deadlines block every week.

## Final Two Weeks and Conference

- [ ] Chase final sponsor payments; this ran into conference week in both 2025 and 2026
- [ ] Registration stays open through the conference, including day-of walk-ups; only a sellout closes it
- [ ] Provide headcounts to venue, catering, and the network event, and refresh them as late sales land
- [ ] Day-of paid swag runs through Pretix voucher QR codes, not cash
- [ ] Keep receipts for supply runs, parking, and tips

## Post-Conference

- [ ] Collect and pay final vendor invoices; the final A/V installment lands about 3.5 weeks after
- [ ] Reconcile the budget sheet: actuals per line, bottom line against target
- [ ] Confirm every sponsorship payment landed
- [ ] Prepare tax records for the treasurer (a profitable year means tax work)
- [ ] Thank sponsors with Marketing and start early outreach to satisfied sponsors for next year
- [ ] Feed finance lessons into the retro doc

## A/V Payment Shape

Deposit at booking, an installment or two near the event, and a final installment after.
2025 paid a deposit about 6 months out, an installment 10 days before the conference, and the final about 3.5 weeks after; 2026 paid the Garner Media deposit about 3 months out.
Whatever schedule the contract sets, every due date goes in the Upcoming Deadlines block until paid.

## If Things Go Wrong (Untested Guidance)

None of these playbooks has been exercised; treat them as starting points, not procedure.

- Sponsor cancels: get it in writing, refund if paid, update the budget sheet, and pull their marketing approvals.
- Pretix outage at the desk: printed attendee list as backup, manual check-in, reconcile afterward.
- Budget shortfall: flag it to the Conference Chair immediately; cut variable spend (catering scale, swag extras) before anything speaker- or attendee-facing.

## Lessons Learned

- 2025: record sponsorship revenue, multiples of any prior year.
- 2025: two sponsors never redeemed their comped tickets; track redemption explicitly.
- 2025: the PSF freeze plus its 8-week lead forced a scramble; apply early.
- 2026: the insurance deadline was met only because it headlined the deadlines block.
- 2026: the lanyard order was held behind the lanyard sponsor's payment; sequence dependent orders.
- 2026: the 501(c)(3) exemption cut a swag quote; remind vendors.
- 2024: a Gold sponsorship closed only because the sponsor's fiscal year ended January 31; know fiscal calendars.
- Every year: payment chasing runs into conference week; invoice promptly and follow up on a schedule.
