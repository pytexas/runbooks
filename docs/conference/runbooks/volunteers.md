# Volunteers Runbook

Owned by Venue Committee.

Day-of volunteers are distinct from organizers (who work months prior).

See `venue-committee-runbook.md`.

## Why Volunteers Matter

Day-of staffing defaults to organizers plus whoever's free.
That barely works at 250 attendees and leaves no slack for rotations or breaks.
Without a published intake form, people walk up at registration asking how to volunteer and a form gets built mid-conference.

## Recruitment (Month -2)

- [ ] Publish a volunteer intake form ahead of the conference (do not wait for walk-ups)
- [ ] Define day-of roles before recruiting (see Specific Roles below)
- [ ] Run a short all-hands before doors open each day

Recruit through:

- PyTexas email to past attendees
- Discord and community channels
- Marketing announcement
- Local Python meetups
- Student organizations
- Personal asks (highest conversion)

Incentives:

- Volunteer shirt or other identifier
- Free or comped conference ticket
- Early swag access

## Shift Planning

Exact counts depend on the year; plan around these fixed points:

- Registration peaks Saturday 8:00-10:00; taper after mid-morning
- The shirt table must stay staffed all day
- A/V and stream monitoring rotates in shifts; never one person all weekend
- Lunch needs a captain per restaurant
- Teardown needs the most hands: Sunday 5:25-5:45, hard out at 7:00

Use 2-3 hour shifts with clear handoffs, and keep a floater or two for coverage and breaks.

## Volunteer Coordinator Role

A central coordinator (Venue lead or designate):

- Owns volunteer recruitment
- Builds the shift schedule
- Briefs volunteers at start of shift
- Handles no-shows and gaps
- Decides on-the-fly reassignments

Station leads (Registration, A/V, Sponsors) run their own briefings.

## Briefing

Before doors open (15-30 min):

- Walk through the role
- Show locations
- Communication channel and escalation path (the phone roster lives in the Org Info Sheet)
- Emergency procedures
- Identifier check (organizers wear staff shirts in the year's organizer color; a second distinct style marks the speaker team)

Print a role description per volunteer:

```
PyTexas [YEAR] - [Day] - [Shift Time]

Volunteer Name:
Role: Registration Desk
Location: Pre-Function Area

Responsibilities:
- Greet attendees
- Help with badge lookup
- Direct to badge pickup
- Answer basic questions
- Escalate issues to your station lead

Contact:
- Station lead: [ROLE] [PHONE]
- Conference Chair: [PHONE]

Thanks for volunteering!
```

## Day-of

Check-in:

- Volunteer arrives 30 min before shift
- Check in with the coordinator
- Get identifier (shirt, lanyard, or ribbon)
- Brief, then take station

Throughout:

- Coordinator does mid-shift check-ins
- Cover bio breaks
- Handle volunteer needs (food, water, breaks)

Shift end:

- Brief the incoming volunteer
- Thank the departing volunteer

## Specific Roles

### Registration

See `registration.md`.

- 2 on check-in laptops
- 1-2 on badge handout
- 1 floater for issues

Skills: friendly, comfortable with software, patient.

### Room Monitor

- Timekeeping for speakers (StageTimer runs the countdown)
- Hand off mic for Q&A
- Help with A/V issues; escalate to the A/V vendor

Skills: comfortable near the stage, can troubleshoot light A/V.

### Floater

- Direct attendees
- Answer questions
- Cover bio breaks for others
- Spot issues

Skills: knows the venue, patient.

### Catering Helper

- Restock at breaks
- Trash management
- Allergen sign placement
- Receive deliveries in their windows

Skills: physical work, attention to dietary detail.

### Shirt Table

- Verify shirt buyers by poker chip redemption
- Hand out shirts by size
- Inventory tracking
- Table must stay staffed; unstaffed tables lose shirts

Skills: organized, friendly.

### Lunch Captain

- Lead a group to an assigned restaurant
- Carry a flag for visibility
- Manage group size at the venue
- Return on time for the 2:00 restart

Skills: outgoing, knows the area, comfortable leading a group.

### Discord Moderator

See `discord.md`. At least one backup must know the streaming and chat workflow.

## Appreciation

Day-of:

- Public thank-you in announcements
- Volunteers eat first and get coffee
- Coordinator checks in regularly

Post-conference (week 1):

- Public thank-you on social
- Personal email per volunteer
- Quick survey: did the role match expectations, what worked, what was confusing, would you volunteer again, want to organize next year
- Optional extra swag

Long-term:

- Track for next year's recruitment
- Invite top performers to organizer onboarding (see `onboarding-new-lead.md`)

## Pitfalls

- More than one person must know how to run check-in.
- Pick one chat tool for day-of coordination; juggling several at once is painful.
