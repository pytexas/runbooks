# Day-of Operations Guide

Run-of-day for Friday setup plus tutorials and the Saturday/Sunday main conference. Pair with `run-of-show.md` for the minute-by-minute.

There is no Thursday venue access.
The venue contract covers three 13-hour days, Friday through Sunday, 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
All setup happens Friday morning, in parallel with tutorials.

## Friday: Setup and Tutorial Day

Tutorials run 9:00-12:00 and 2:00-5:00, with a 90-minute sponsored workshop at 4:30.
Registration is open 9:00-12:00 and 2:00-5:00.
Friday is the light program day; use it to stage everything the weekend needs.

### Early Morning (6:00-9:00)

- [ ] 6:00 venue access; Conference Chair arrives at open
- [ ] 7:15 coffee pickup
- [ ] Friday breakfast out (Central Market muffins, fruit, coffee boxes); buy light Friday to gauge preferences, then bulk-buy for the weekend
- [ ] 8:00 registration setup, food setup, linens layout
- [ ] Speaker tech checks for anyone on site before 9:00 (the other window is lunch)

Registration desk setup:

- [ ] 3-table bubble layout, room for backpack drop
- [ ] 2 laptops on check-in, power strip, label printer, backup lists, pens
- [ ] Badges sorted alphabetically; blank badges staged for late registrants
- [ ] Lanyards on stands, separate from badges
- [ ] Numbered signs for the three-station flow: (1) intake form, (2) badge pickup, (3) shirt pickup by poker chip

Badge and shirt prep:

- [ ] Bundle shirts with badges per buyer; bag shirts
- [ ] Poker chips into badge bags for shirt buyers
- [ ] Ribbons (meetup organizer, hiring, etc.) into bags if used
- [ ] Shirt table sorted by size with size signs; keep it staffed

Tutorial room:

- [ ] Power strips at every table (a repeat complaint in 2025 and 2026)
- [ ] Test outlets, mics, projector, timer
- [ ] Water out for instructors

Catering:

- [ ] Coffee station, allergen signs, plates and napkins
- [ ] Trash cans in multiple spots
- [ ] See `catering.md` for the plan sheet and delivery windows

Signage:

- [ ] Standing signs and single-page signs placed per the Miro board map
- [ ] "Badge required for entry" on doors; "please wear your badge" signs
- [ ] "On Deck" table sign near the stage
- [ ] Lunch signs (restaurant, walking time, dietary notes, captain)

### During the Morning Tutorial

- [ ] Costco + HEB supply run: snacks, sodas, misc (ice is ordered online)
- [ ] Late check-ins at the registration desk
- [ ] Check on instructors and A/V periodically
- [ ] Restock at breaks, empty trash

### End of Day

- [ ] Thank instructors
- [ ] Pack leftovers, secure equipment and swag
- [ ] Brief the Saturday team

### Friday Evening: Appreciation Dinner

Invite-only dinner for speakers, sponsors, organizers, and Friends of PyTexas.
This replaced the separate speaker dinner; 2026 held it on the library rooftop, catered by Terry Black's.

- [ ] Brief speakers in person here (they do not read emails): green room location, on-deck flow, tech-check windows
- [ ] Confirm RSVPs against the headcount given to the caterer

## Saturday and Sunday: Main Days

### Daily Rhythm

- 6:00 venue access
- 7:15 coffee pickup
- 7:30 food delivery (tacos Saturday, kolaches Sunday)
- 8:00 registration opens; food setup; everything set by 8:00 exactly
- 8:55 chair sets up opening remarks; sponsor chaser rounds up pitch presenters
- 9:00 opening remarks (10 min)
- 9:10 sponsor pitches by tier (Platinum Saturday, Gold/Silver Sunday)
- 9:20 keynote (~45 min)
- Talks in 30-minute blocks: 25-minute talk plus 5 minutes of combined Q&A and next-speaker setup
- 20-minute sponsored talk immediately before lunch (Platinum benefit)
- Lunch 12:30-2:00, not catered: organizer-led group walks
- Central Market afternoon-break delivery ~2:25-2:55
- Door prizes scattered through the day: after the keynote, before lunch, mid-afternoon, end of day; entry via Google Form
- Saturday close: sponsored workshop 4:30-6:00, 30 minutes of lightning talks, door prizes, depart for the network event ~6:35
- Sunday close: lightning talks ~4:45, closing remarks with door prizes ~5:25, cleanup and out by 5:45 (hard out at 7:00)

## Troubleshooting

Registration:

- App down: use printed backup, sync later
- Person not in system: try email variations and name search; worst case, blank badge and label printer
- Bot fails on bulk corporate purchase (registering email vs attendee email): manually add the Discord role

A/V:

- Projector won't display: try Dell/Apple/HP dongles; USB-C preferred; fall back to the speaker's screen
- Mic down: hot swap, batteries, A/V vendor
- Stream drops: library bandwidth dips have happened; hotspot failover was judged impractical in 2025, so ride it out and note timestamps for the vendor
- Timer dies: phone running stagetimer.io

Catering:

- Delivery late: delay start; always order with a 10+ minute buffer
- Running short: emergency Costco or HEB run
- Allergens: signs pre-made, separate trays

Speakers:

- Late or missing: phone tree, shuffle slots, Python trivia filler
- Tech broken: backup slides on an organizer laptop
- Running over: 5/2/wrap warnings, cut Q&A
- Never let the schedule run early; have filler ready

Venue:

- Hallway too loud: close doors during talks; sound deadening is a standing open problem

## Lessons Learned

- 2025: "Don't panic at the conference chair until at least day two."
- Lanyards on stands, separate from badges. Tangled lanyards bottleneck registration.
- 2 people on check-in laptops, 1-2 on badges. Pre-bundle shirts with badges on Friday.
- Power strips at every tutorial table (complaint in both 2025 and 2026).
- People miss the second food line. Sign or volunteer to direct.
- Lunch destinations must be decided and signed before the conference. Captains per restaurant.
- Speakers do not read emails. Brief them in person at the Friday dinner.
- A/V monitoring needs shifts, not one person all weekend.
- Order ice online; it worked well in 2025.
- Scattered door prizes with Google Form entry work. The wheel-of-names was retired after 2025.
- People forget badges on day 2. Stock bright loaner lanyards.
- A printed phone roster beats chat tools in emergencies.
- 40-50 lb of bananas go across the weekend (2024 and 2025 counts).
