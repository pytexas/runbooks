# Code of Conduct Runbook

The policy of record is the PyTexas Code of Conduct published on pytexas.org.
This page covers operations: how reports come in, who responds, and how incidents are handled.
It does not restate or replace the policy text.

Owned by Community Committee; the Conference Chair is the escalation point.

## Before the Conference

- [ ] Confirm the CoC is current and linked from the website, the ticket flow, and the Attendee KBYG
- [ ] Identify the response team: multiple trained responders, not one person
- [ ] Brief responders on the reporting flow, the enforcement ladder below, and documentation
- [ ] Put responder phone numbers in the Org Info Sheet roster
- [ ] Confirm sponsor contracts bind sponsor representatives to the CoC (the standard sponsor agreement does this, and the Foundation holds a reputational-risk termination clause)
- [ ] Brief Discord moderators on CoC handling and escalation

## Reporting Channels Day-Of

Attendees can report through any of these:

- Any organizer in a staff shirt (the year's organizer color), in person
- A direct message to a Discord moderator or organizer
- The contact channel published in the CoC itself

Whoever receives a report brings it to a response-team member immediately.
Serious incidents go straight to the Conference Chair.
The report-taker does not adjudicate on the spot; they document and hand off.

## Enforcement Ladder

Minor (mild inappropriate comment):

1. Warn privately, explain the issue
2. Document
3. Monitor

Moderate (persistent or hostile behavior):

1. Serious private conversation
2. Mute or timeout in Discord if online
3. Document thoroughly, inform the Conference Chair
4. Ask the person to leave if in person and the behavior continues

Serious (harassment, discrimination, threats):

1. Immediate removal from the venue or ban from the server
2. Inform the Conference Chair immediately
3. Venue security if needed; police if criminal
4. Document everything
5. Follow up with the person who was targeted
6. Ban from future events

This ladder applies equally to attendees, speakers, volunteers, organizers, and sponsor representatives.

## Incident Documentation

Fill this out for every report, including ones resolved with a quiet word:

```
Date/time:
Location (channel or physical):
Reported by:
Reported to:
People involved:
Description:
CoC violation(s):
Action taken:
Follow-up needed:
Reported to Conference Chair: Y/N
Police contacted: Y/N
Notes:
```

Records go to the Conference Chair and are kept out of shared committee folders.

## After the Conference

- [ ] Confirm every incident record is complete and any follow-up done
- [ ] Follow up with reporters on outcomes where appropriate
- [ ] Bring a count and any process lessons (not identifying details) to the retro
- [ ] Update this page and the response-team briefing with what was learned

## Cross-Committee

- Finance: CoC language in sponsor contracts
- Speaker: CoC expectations in speaker comms and the speaker KBYG
- Community: Discord moderation, day-of visibility
- Conference Chair: escalation, records, final calls on removal or bans
