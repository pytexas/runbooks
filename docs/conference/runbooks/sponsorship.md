# Sponsorship Runbook

Owned by the Finance Committee.
All sponsor contact runs through sponsorship@pytexas.org.
See `finance-committee-runbook.md` for the full finance lifecycle and `run-of-show.md` for pitch-slot scheduling.

## Timeline

- [ ] Month -7 (September): finalize the prospectus so it publishes by early October. Corporate budget season is the reason; slipping past October loses deals.
- [ ] Month -7: confirm a draft run of show with pitch-slot skeleton exists before the prospectus ships, so pitch slots can be written into contracts. See `run-of-show.md`.
- [ ] Month -6.5 (early October): publish the prospectus and email it to the year's sponsor Google Group.
- [ ] Month -5.5 (late October): clone per-tier contract templates from last year into the year's Sponsor Templates folder in Drive.
- [ ] Month -4 (mid-December): prepare the year's invoice template.
- [ ] October through conference: run outreach, contracts, invoicing, and payment chasing continuously. Review per-sponsor status at every planning meeting.
- [ ] Week -5: publish the Sponsor KBYG and build the sponsor marketing sheet with the Approved To Market gate.
- [ ] Weeks -2 to 0: sponsor countdown posts run daily (Marketing executes; Finance supplies the approved list).
- [ ] Conference week: chase final payments. This has run into conference week every year; plan for it.
- [ ] Post-conference: thank-yous, comp-ticket redemption check, renewal outreach.

## Tier Structure

The 2026 prospectus sold four standard tiers and four specialty tiers.
Quantities are caps, not targets.

| Tier | Available | Distinguishing benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 2 | 4 tickets plus 10 discounted corporate tickets, pre-keynote pitch Saturday, premium table placement, 20-minute sponsored talk right before lunch |
| Gold | 5 | 2 tickets plus 5 discounted corporate tickets, short pitch Saturday, sponsor-hall table |
| Silver | 5 | 1 ticket plus 2 discounted corporate tickets, short pitch Sunday, back-of-hall table |
| Bronze/Community | 15 | 1 ticket, 1-minute pitch during Saturday lightning talks, popup banner option |
| Lanyard | 2 | Logo on the conference lanyard |
| Captioning | 2 | Logo on all-day captioning and on the permanent talk videos |
| Food/Coffee | 3 | Logo at food tables for one day or the coffee bar both days |
| Network Event | 4 | Table at the Saturday evening event |

Also sold: bulk corporate ticket packages (10+ at a corporate discount).
These work as a foot in the door for companies without a sponsorship budget; track buyers for next year's outreach.

Every sponsor at every tier gets: a social media callout, website logo, morning thank-you slide, space on the shared swag table, and a job-board posting.

The Platinum sponsored talk is subject to the normal review process.
Sales pitches are rejected, and the prospectus says so explicitly; accepting them risks the Foundation's 501(c)(3) status.

Booth assignment is first come, first served within tier placement rules.
An hourly demo space was priced and offered in the 2026 cycle and then dropped; the demo area never sells out, and the 2027 venue booking excludes it.

## Prospectus

- [ ] Rebuild from last year's deck: tiers, benefits, quantities, process.
- [ ] Update demographics slides from last year's registration intake data.
- [ ] Include the check-in rate; PyTexas runs well above the community-event norm and it sells.
- [ ] Confirm pitch slots against the run of show draft before printing benefits.
- [ ] Finance reviews, Conference Chair approves.
- [ ] Publish by early October, save to the year's Drive folder, and email the sponsor Google Group.

## Recruitment

Past sponsors renew at the highest rate; start there.

- [ ] Email past sponsors through the year's Google Group with a short personalized note and the prospectus.
- [ ] Create next year's Google Group of sponsor contacts as contacts appear.
- [ ] Publish an employee template letter in the newsletter so community members can pitch their own employers.
- [ ] Know each prospect's fiscal-year end and time the ask to it. 2024: a Gold closed only because its budget expired January 31.
- [ ] Post the prospectus on LinkedIn and the Foundation's accounts (historically low conversion; still worth the presence).

Companies that have sponsored recently, as target-list context: Microsoft, Temporal, and Anaconda at Platinum; Elastic at Gold; Cloudflare at Silver; Bloomberg (lanyard, plus coffee in 2025); GitButler (network event); uStudio and Caktus Group (captioning, 2025); Black Python Devs at the community level; devIT and Pretix as media and tech sponsors.
The PSF grant is treated as sponsorship revenue in the budget; see `grants.md`.
Expect long pursuits that go nowhere: one large prospect was worked for months in the 2026 cycle and declined.

## Contracts

- [ ] Clone per-tier agreement templates from last year (~5.5 months out): one per standard and specialty tier.
- [ ] Send through Docusign; the Secretary signs first.
- [ ] A countersigned contract is required before anything else moves.
- [ ] Create a per-sponsor folder in the year's Drive Sponsors folder for the contract and invoices.

Contract terms that matter in practice:

- Payment is net 30 from the effective date.
- Benefits are secured only on full payment.
- The Code of Conduct binds sponsor representatives.
- The Foundation holds a unilateral reputational-risk termination clause.
- Materials deadlines and booth assignment rules live in the tier exhibit.

## Invoicing and Payment

- [ ] Invoice from the year's template immediately after countersignature.
- [ ] Payment options: Stripe credit card, EFT, or purchase order.
- [ ] Track per-sponsor status at every meeting: contract state, invoice sent, paid.
- [ ] Follow up on unpaid invoices every meeting until closed.
- [ ] Sequence dependent orders behind payment. 2026: the lanyard order was deliberately held until the lanyard sponsor paid.
- [ ] Track that sponsors actually redeem their comped tickets. 2025: two never did.
- [ ] Remind sponsors to redeem ticket vouchers early if they want Network Event space.

Payment chasing has run into conference week every year.
Budget standing time for it and never let a benefit ship ahead of the money.

## Approved to Market Gate

Sponsors get zero promotion until payment is confirmed.

- [ ] Build the sponsor marketing sheet (~5 weeks out): one row per sponsor with tier, paid status, an "Approved To Market?" column, logo, canned promo copy, scheduled post date, and per-platform post links.
- [ ] Marketing posts only rows with the approval flag set. An unpaid sponsor simply never gets a post.
- [ ] The contract obligates one social shoutout; practice is one before the conference and one during.
- [ ] Schedule countdown posts across the final two weeks, one sponsor per slot.

## Sponsor Know Before You Go

Publish about 5 weeks out to every signed sponsor.
Contents that must be in it:

- Venue address, parking, and Wi-Fi.
- Check-in windows: Friday morning and afternoon, or Saturday 7-9 AM.
- Exhibit hours and teardown flexibility.
- Booth spec: 5-foot table, two chairs, power.
- Shipping: ship to your hotel; the venue accepts no deliveries.
- No lead scanners; point to lead-gathering alternatives.
- Shirt-order deadline (~5 weeks out) for guaranteed day-of pickup.
- Meals: breakfast provided, lunch on your own.

## Day-Of

- [ ] Assign a sponsor liaison for the weekend.
- [ ] Put a "sponsor chaser" in the run of show each morning to round up pitch presenters before opening remarks.
- [ ] Check on sponsors twice daily (also in the run of show).
- [ ] Verify logos on the thank-you slide and the shared swag table setup.

## Post-Conference

- [ ] Thank-you email to every sponsor within the first week.
- [ ] Confirm every contracted benefit was delivered; note misses.
- [ ] Feed registration intake demographics to next year's prospectus.
- [ ] Renewal touch 1-2 months later with next year's dates.

## Lessons Learned

- 2026: publish the prospectus by early October; "no later than end of next month because it's budget season" is the standing rule.
- 2025, 2026: payment chasing runs to conference week. Sequence dependent orders behind payment.
- 2025: track comp-ticket redemption; two sponsors never used theirs.
- 2025: build the run of show before selling sponsorships so pitch slots can be spaced and written into contracts. Sold, scheduled pitch spots were adopted into the 2026 tier design.
- 2025: a specialty tier can be invented mid-cycle when a cost needs covering; captioning was split across two sponsors this way.
- 2025 retro: consider dropping Bronze; watch table space if the sponsor count grows.
- 2026: the per-year sponsor Google Group works; keep it current all year.
- 2026: the demo space never sells out; stop offering it.
- 2024: know prospects' fiscal-year ends; one Gold closed only because its budget was about to expire.
