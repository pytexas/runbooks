# Network Event Runbook

Venue Committee owns this.
Covers both evening events: the Saturday network event (open to attendees as a ticket add-on) and the Friday appreciation dinner (invite-only).
See `venue-committee-runbook.md` and `catering.md`.

## Saturday Network Event

Saturday evening, about 3 hours (6:00-9:00 PM pattern), roughly 150 guests.
Renamed from "After Party" to "Networking Event" at the 2025 kickoff.
Together with food and beverage, this is one of the largest expense categories in the budget.

### Two Documented Models

**Off-site bar buyout (2025, Remedy).**
Contract structure: hourly venue buyout plus an hourly food-and-beverage minimum plus 20% gratuity on F&B; non-refundable deposit at signing, balance due at the event, cancellation tiers at 14 days.
Bar model: attendees get fixed-value drink tickets (combinable; overages paid by the attendee); organizers monitor the tab during the night and release more tickets if spend is tracking under the F&B minimum.
Food: rolling waves of shareable bar food (pizzas, sliders, chips and queso, pretzel bites, brownies); start with a few of everything, reorder as items run out.
The venue handles alcohol compliance.

**Self-run rooftop (2026, library Roof Garden).**
La Pera catered the food; alcohol was self-supplied from Costco; bartenders were hired separately (Drink Slingers and Elevate both quoted).
Self-running alcohol service means the Foundation carries the compliance burden: a TABC permit and a hired licensed peace officer (city requirement; two officers budgeted).
Extra logistics: two 8-foot tablecloths for the bar, cooler and leftover-alcohol plans, and rooftop access coordination with the library.

**2027 reverts to an established off-site venue.**
The rooftop was judged a headache ("roof is turning into a headache already; rather go back to an established place for after party next year").
Default to the buyout model unless there is a strong reason not to.

### Alcohol Compliance (Self-Run Model Only)

- [ ] Submit the TABC permit application months out (2026 submitted about 2.5 weeks out and it was still in review the day before the event)
- [ ] Secure a licensed peace officer early; hiring one took a month of bounced emails in 2026 and only closed through a personal contact
- [ ] Arrange officer payment logistics in advance, not on the day
- [ ] Plan who buys and transports the alcohol (2026: organizers bought from Costco); confirm the bartending vendor's scope in the quote
- [ ] Fallback position, stated in the 2026 minutes: "worst case no alcohol"

### Timeline

- [ ] Kickoff to Month -6: build the candidate venue list
- [ ] Weeks -11 to -8: get quotes, tour, and book (2025 booked its bar about 11 weeks out)
- [ ] Week -8.5: confirm headcount (~150) and deposit amounts
- [ ] Week -6.5: pay the deposit (roughly half of the total)
- [ ] Weeks -5 to -4: peace officer and TABC work if self-running alcohol (start the officer hunt months earlier)
- [ ] Week -2.5: finalize the event plan
- [ ] Day -1: deliver any marquee or screen content the venue requested

### Ticketing and Door

- [ ] Sell the event as a Pretix ticket add-on; free entry classes exist for sponsors and organizer comps
- [ ] Network Event tier sponsors get a table; their ticket vouchers must be redeemed early to reserve space
- [ ] Check tickets at the door; cover every entrance
- [ ] Stand up a waitlist form on sellout; the 2025 flow offered either an immediate paid add via invoice, or an email-confirmation option where the org may call 8 AM-6 PM and the person has 4 hours to accept or forfeit
- [ ] The add-on sells strongly (2025 sold about 134 of 150); expect a waitlist

### Day-Of

- [ ] Arrive 30-60 minutes before doors; brief venue staff, bartenders, and volunteers
- [ ] Set up the door check with the attendee list ready
- [ ] Monitor capacity and the F&B tab (buyout model) through the night
- [ ] Take photos; thank sponsors present
- [ ] Plan leftovers, coolers, and organizer transport home before the event starts
- [ ] Confirm everyone is out, settle bills, and do a final walkthrough with the venue

## Friday Appreciation Dinner

The Friday dinner is its own institution, distinct from the Saturday event.

- Invite-only: speakers, sponsors, organizers, and Friends of PyTexas; about 50-75 guests.
- 2026 held it on the library rooftop, catered by Terry Black's, with a bar.
- It replaced the separate speaker dinner; since 2026 the speaker event and the thank-you dinner are one Friday event.
- Speakers are briefed in person here (speakers do not reliably read email); the speaker KBYG carries the RSVP-count reminder.

Checklist:

- [ ] Week -11: set the headcount assumption (50-75) in the catering ideas doc
- [ ] Weeks -10 to -8: get quotes at two headcount variants
- [ ] Week -6.5: pay the deposit (roughly half of the total)
- [ ] Weeks -2 to -1: collect dietary restrictions from speakers and guests (2026 did this the final week; do it earlier)
- [ ] Week -1: confirm final headcount from speaker and sponsor RSVPs
- [ ] At the dinner: brief speakers in person on day-of logistics

The 2026 minutes describe this dinner as the event that "can be nicer"; it is the thank-you to the people who make the conference run.
The 2026 Terry Black's line ran well over its budget line, so size the budget honestly.

## Cross-Committee

- Finance: contracts, deposits, add-on ticket pricing, Network Event sponsor tier
- Marketing: event promotion, sellout and waitlist messaging
- Speaker: dinner invites and RSVP counts via the speaker KBYG
- Community: pre-event Discord buildup
- Conference Chair: sponsor thanks at opening remarks

## Lessons Learned

- 2026: self-running alcohol (TABC permit plus peace officer) is long-lead and fragile; start months out and expect bounced contacts.
- 2026: the rooftop model was judged a headache; 2027 returns to an established off-site venue.
- 2025: the bar buyout model priced predictably (buyout plus F&B minimum plus 20% gratuity) and the venue carried compliance.
- 2025: the space felt cramped at entry despite empty front tables; walk the room layout before booking.
- 2025: feedback asked to move networking earlier in the day; one floated idea is converting Saturday's last two hours to networking/open spaces and bringing back a sponsor-hosted after party where attendees buy their own food and drink.
- 2026: the networking event's food was limiting for vegetarians and non-beef/pork eaters; order for dietary variety as well as volume.
