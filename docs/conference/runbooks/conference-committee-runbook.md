# Conference Committee Runbook

Chair: Conference Chair (the Foundation President is the Conference Chair).

Responsibilities: overall coordination, committee oversight, meeting management, next-year planning, risk, escalation.

See `conference-master-timeline.md`, `kickoff-meeting.md`, `next-year-planning.md`.

## Standing Practices

### Meeting Discipline

- [ ] 45-minute target, hard cutoff at one hour, per-section timers in the agenda
- [ ] Fixed committee report order: Chair, Speaker, Finance, Marketing, Venue, Community
- [ ] 5-minute update slot per committee, split into "discussion for this time" and "items for next meeting"
- [ ] Action items captured with owners and due dates
- [ ] One running minutes doc for the whole cycle, newest meeting on top, reusable template at the bottom

Cadence ramps with the calendar: roughly monthly after kickoff, biweekly from January, weekly from about 9 weeks out.
Fixed report order, per-section timers, and special sessions keep a small organizing team on one page inside an hour.

### Upcoming Deadlines Block

Keep a standing Upcoming Deadlines section in every agenda listing hard payment and order dates: insurance, catering deposits, shirt order close, TABC permit, badges, sponsor payments.

Rolling-template minutes lose action items without it.
Small items stay "next week's problem" for weeks, and hard deadlines like insurance hold only when they headline this block.

### Special Sessions

Split large decision work out of the regular meeting into dedicated special sessions so the regular meeting keeps its timers.
Typical special sessions: opportunity grant review (the day after applications close) and catering deposits and headcounts.

### Next-Year Planning

Next-year planning runs inside the current cycle, not after it.
Dates are conflict-checked about 11 weeks before the current conference, the deposit goes down about 9 weeks out, and the venue contract is signed right after the current conference at the latest.
See `next-year-planning.md`.

## Month -13 to -12: During and Just After the Previous Conference

- [ ] Sign next year's venue agreement and pay the deposit (see `next-year-planning.md`)
- [ ] Create the new cycle's budget sheet and retro doc; the retro doc is live from day one
- [ ] Launch the post-event survey on the last conference day (see `post-event.md`)
- [ ] Hold the retro meeting within 2 weeks

## Month -12 to -9

- [ ] Refine the retro; carry action items into the new cycle
- [ ] Review committee structure and identify gaps
- [ ] Recruit organizers and candidate leads
- [ ] Track logo work with Marketing (starts around Month -12 to -10)

## Month -8 (Mid-August): Kickoff

The kickoff is virtual, at Month -8 (mid-August).
See `kickoff-meeting.md` for the full template.

- [ ] Stand up the year's Important Documents: minutes doc, Drive folder, budget sheet, retro doc
- [ ] Assign committee leads via round-robin volunteer mapping
- [ ] Set annual goals: sell-out date, profit target, meeting discipline, process goals
- [ ] Set the CFP open date (October 1) as the cycle's first hard deadline
- [ ] Set up task tracking (Asana) and the chat platform
- [ ] Set meeting cadence and clone the agenda template

Six units, each with a lead:

1. Conference Chair (coordination, meetings, next-year planning, escalation)
2. Speaker (CFP, talk selection, keynotes, tutorials, schedule, speaker comms)
3. Finance (budget, sponsorship, registration money, grants, insurance, taxes)
4. Marketing (social campaigns, newsletter, website, design assets)
5. Venue (venue relationship, A/V, catering, evening events, swag, badges, registration desk, volunteers)
6. Community (outreach, Discord, streaming, virtual experience, aftercare)

## Month -8 to -6

- [ ] Monitor CFP launch with Speaker (opens October 1)
- [ ] Monitor prospectus publication with Finance (early October, to hit corporate budget season)
- [ ] Monitor website launch with Marketing (live before October 1 for the newsletter)
- [ ] Confirm A/V vendor outreach with Venue
- [ ] Keep runbooks updated as decisions land; do not wait until after the conference

## Month -6 to -4

- [ ] Monitor early bird launch (December 1) with Finance and Venue
- [ ] Monitor CFP close and talk review with Speaker (early December, review over the holidays)
- [ ] Track ticket pacing against the sell-out goal; push marketing earlier if behind
- [ ] Choose next year's dates and target a signed next-year contract by February 1 (see `next-year-planning.md`)

## Month -4 to -2

- [ ] Weekly status reviews across committees; resolve blockers immediately
- [ ] Run the grant review special session (applications close ~Week -8.5)
- [ ] Run the catering special session (deposits ~Week -8.5)
- [ ] Budget watch with Finance
- [ ] Keep the Upcoming Deadlines block current: insurance (Week -4.5), shirt pre-order close (~Week -5), TABC permit, badges
- [ ] Confirm day-of roles and prevent organizer burnout

## Month -2 to Conference

- [ ] Weekly all-hands
- [ ] Build the run of show final grid ~2 weeks out; a pitch-slot draft should have existed since before the prospectus shipped (see `run-of-show.md`)
- [ ] Backup coverage for critical roles, including streaming
- [ ] Brief volunteers
- [ ] Confirm the phone-numbers roster in the Org Info Sheet; do not rely only on chat in emergencies
- [ ] Emergency kit: gaffer tape, extension cords, markers, paper, first aid, chargers
- [ ] Review final attendee emails with Community

## Conference Days

- [ ] Arrive at venue access (6:00 AM); the venue closes hard at 7:00 PM
- [ ] Morning huddle with organizers
- [ ] 8:55 set up opening remarks; deliver them at 9:00
- [ ] Monitor operations and schedule adherence
- [ ] Handle escalations and executive decisions
- [ ] Check in with each committee during the day
- [ ] End-of-day debrief, brief the team on the next day, thank the team

Delegation is the goal.
The chair should not be the only one handling everything.
"Don't panic at the conference chair until at least day two."

## Post-Conference

- [ ] Survey live from the last conference day (see `post-event.md`)
- [ ] Public thanks: committees, sponsors, speakers, attendees
- [ ] Retro meeting within 2 weeks; the retro doc has been accumulating all year
- [ ] Update runbooks with lessons
- [ ] Confirm the next cycle's Important Documents exist (they should, per `next-year-planning.md`)
- [ ] Hand off to next year's chair if changing

## Escalation Paths

- Any issue: the relevant committee lead first, then the Conference Chair as the escalation point
- Medical: 911, then notify the Conference Chair

## Pitfalls

- The run of show grid slips to 3-4 days out unless it is scheduled; build it 2 weeks out, with a pitch-slot draft in hand before sponsorships are sold.
- Ticket sales lag the sell-out goal unless marketing pushes start early and hit hard; track pacing from the first sale.
- The conference chair is a single point of failure. Delegate harder than feels comfortable.
- Volunteers need defined roles, not "whoever's free."
- Document during the cycle, not after. Runbooks written after the conference lose the detail.
