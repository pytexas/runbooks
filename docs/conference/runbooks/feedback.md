# Attendee Feedback Runbook

Owned by Community Committee, with Marketing promoting the survey and Speaker Committee distributing per-speaker feedback.

See `post-event.md` for the wider post-conference sequence and `community-committee-runbook.md` for aftercare.

## Post-Event Survey

### Build (Weeks -2 to -1)
- [ ] Build the survey before the conference; it launches on the last day, so it cannot be a post-event task
- [ ] Cover: attendance mode, ratings (overall, organization, pre-event info, keynotes, talks, food, venue, networking event), value-for-ticket, recommend and return scores, free-text favorites and least-favorites
- [ ] Add forward-looking questions the committees actually need answered (format experiments, topic demand, swag interest)
- [ ] Keep it 5-10 minutes; mostly optional questions
- [ ] Draft the distribution email and a Discord post

### Launch and Collect
- [ ] Launch on the last conference day (2026 practice); mention it in closing remarks
- [ ] Send the survey email and post in Discord the same day
- [ ] Reminder at one week; final reminder 2 days before close
- [ ] Response window: about 2 weeks
- [ ] Expect roughly 50 responses; that has been typical two years running, so write questions worth 50 careful answers rather than chasing volume

### Analyze
- [ ] Compile ratings and free-text themes into a summary: strengths, recurring criticisms, action items
- [ ] Share with all committees and the Conference Chair
- [ ] Feed the retro doc; the retro meeting lands within ~2 weeks of the conference

## Per-Speaker Feedback

- [ ] Export Slido Q&A and reactions per talk
- [ ] Merge with feedback-form responses about each talk
- [ ] Compile into a per-speaker feedback doc (2026 used a "Speaker Feedback Links" doc in the year's Drive folder)
- [ ] Distribute to each speaker in the weeks after the conference

Speakers rarely get usable feedback from conferences; this is cheap and appreciated.

## What Feedback Drives

The 2026 survey is a good example of what the responses change:

- Too many AI-adjacent talks ("rare to go 15 minutes without someone mentioning AI") fed talk-selection balance for the next cycle.
- Sponsor tutorials read as rushed or sales-y; that feeds sponsored-content review standards.
- Single-track crowding, scarce seating, and hallway-space complaints feed venue and layout planning.
- Strong demand for more networking formats (roundtables, breakouts, a women-of-Python gathering, mentoring) feeds Community's format planning.

Carry themes like these into the retro with their year attached so future organizers can judge staleness.

## Emergency-Contact Data

- [ ] Emergency-contact info is collected from attendees at registration intake
- [ ] Delete it after the conference; it exists for day-of safety only
- [ ] Confirm deletion as a post-event checklist item

## Cross-Committee

- Marketing: survey promotion, feedback-form creation timing (flag it by Week -2; in 2026 it was still a to-do 1.5 weeks out)
- Speaker: per-speaker feedback distribution
- Conference Chair: survey summary feeds the retro
