# Onboarding a New Committee Lead

There is no formal chair training program; this checklist is the closest thing that exists.
It generalizes the documented pattern from the 2026 marketing lead onboarding, where the incoming lead and the Conference Chair built a self-onboarding doc together on their first call.
Prior-year artifacts plus the retros are the curriculum.

## Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Read your area's folder in the prior year's Drive artifacts (Marketing, Networking Event, Speakers, Sponsors, Venue, Run of Show, Speaker Gifts, Swag). The Speakers folder is explicitly marked as reusable as a base for this year; treat the rest the same way.
- [ ] Read the last two retro docs; a single year hides recurring problems.
- [ ] Learn the hard deadlines for your area from the master timeline (CFP opens October 1, prospectus published early October, early bird opens December 1, insurance by Week -4.5, and so on). See `conference-master-timeline.md`.
- [ ] Write your own expectations doc: what you own, your milestone dates, what you need from other committees, and what you still need to learn. Store it in the year's Drive folder.
- [ ] Get tool access early: shared 1Password, the year's Drive folder, the task tracker, Canva, and anything specific to your area. Check seat and sharing limits; the 2026 team's Miro board could not accept members outside the original team, so either one person owns updates or the board gets duplicated to an account that can share.
- [ ] Give a 5-minute committee update at every planning meeting. The update slot is the accountability mechanism; there is no other check-in.

## Notes for the Conference Chair

* The kickoff meeting doubles as recruiting: volunteers are mapped to committees round-robin ("what do YOU want to do").
* Point every new lead at this page plus their role page under `../roles/`.
* If a lead joins mid-cycle, walk them through the Upcoming Deadlines block in the minutes on their first call so nothing hard-dated slips during handoff.
