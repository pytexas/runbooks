# Next-Year Planning Runbook

Owned by the Conference Chair.

Next-year planning is a standing workstream inside the current planning cycle.
By the time the current conference ends, next year's dates are announced, the venue contract is signed, the deposit is paid, the budget sheet exists, and the retro doc is live.

See `conference-committee-runbook.md`, `venue.md`, `post-event.md`.

## Timeline

Offsets are relative to the current conference (mid-April).

### Month -6 to -5 (October, November)

- [ ] [Chair] Gather venue pricing intel for next year and raise it in a planning meeting; library pricing can jump sharply year over year
- [ ] [Chair] Ask organizers for alternative venue ideas; default is staying at the library if nothing better surfaces

### Week -11 (Late January)

- [ ] [Chair] Choose next year's dates by conflict-checking the Austin events spreadsheet
- [ ] [Chair] Confirm the venue can hold those dates

The spreadsheet tracks Easter, Passover, Texas Relays, Cap10k, the kite festival, SXSW proximity, and graduations.
Some conflicts are unavoidable; pick the least bad weekend and note the known overlap in the minutes.

### Week -10 to -9 (Early February)

- [ ] [Chair] Signed next-year venue contract in hand; the standing target is February 1
- [ ] [Chair] Pay the next-year deposit about 9 weeks before the current conference
- [ ] [Chair] Decide scope while booking: which spaces to include (event center, rooftop, demo area); every extra space adds operational overhead

If the contract slips past February, sign immediately after the current conference at the latest.
Agreements typically get signed 12-14 months out.

Venue payment structure: a deposit of roughly half the total at signing, with a 14-day return deadline on the signed agreement.
The balance is due about 3 months before the event.
Cancellation refund tiers step down at 91+, 31-90, and 30 days.

### Week -8 to -4 (February, March)

- [ ] [Chair] Theme brainstorm in a regular meeting (~7.5 weeks out)
- [ ] [Chair] Pick next year's theme and logo direction (~4.5 weeks out)
- [ ] [Chair/Marketing] Line up a graphic designer for the logo; production runs Month -12 to -10 of the next cycle so the logo is done before kickoff
- [ ] [Chair] Confirm next cycle's coordination tooling (organizing runs on Discord with a ticket-purchase webhook)

### Conference Weekend

- [ ] [Finance] Create next year's budget sheet during the current conference weekend, ideally on day 1
- [ ] [Chair] Create next cycle's retro doc; it is live from day one and accumulates all year
- [ ] [Marketing] Announce next year's dates at the current conference; announce with a draft page, do not build the full site during the event

### Weeks +1 to +2

- [ ] [Chair] Countersign and return the venue agreement if not already done; pay the deposit within the 14-day window
- [ ] [Chair] Fold retro action items into the new cycle's planning (see `post-event.md`)

## Why This Runs In-Cycle

The venue calendar fills more than a year out, and the library's pricing and terms shift year to year.
Signing early locks the dates, the rate, and the announcement at the current conference.
Waiting until after the conference means announcing "dates TBD" to a room full of attendees who plan travel.

## Pitfalls

- Do not build the full next-year site during the event; announce dates with a draft page and finish the site later.
- Extra venue spaces look attractive on paper and cost real operational attention on the day; book only what the program needs.
- The budget sheet and retro doc existing before kickoff is what lets the kickoff run in 45 minutes; see `kickoff-meeting.md`.
