    Venue Runbook¶

    Owned by Venue Committee.

    See venue-committee-runbook.md for the full operations workflow and av.md for A/V vendor details.

    Current Venue: Austin Central Library¶

    Address: 710 W Cesar Chavez St, Austin, TX 78701 Map: library.austintexas.gov/node/1734558

    Spaces¶

    Special Event Center (main, 1st floor): - 4,450 sq ft; 250 is the sell-out number, stretchable to 275; theater max 350 - Use: talks, keynotes; Pre-Function Area for registration and sponsor tables - Green room, private restrooms - Library provides furniture but no linens (rent from American Party Rental)

    Access hours: - 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Friday through Sunday - There is no Thursday access; setup happens Friday morning in parallel with tutorials

    Rooftop Garden: - Evening hours (6:00 PM to midnight) - Not part of the standard booking; the evening events default to off-site venues (see network-event.md) - Running rooftop events adds logistics burden (equipment security, after-hours access, weather backup); book it only with a strong reason

    Kitchen: - Cold storage available - No full cooking facilities - Catering brought in - Coordinate access with vendor

    First floor demo space: - Billed hourly with a 2-hour minimum - Not part of the standard booking; the demo area never sells out as a sponsor add-on, so skip it

    Security¶

    Assess and budget for equipment security whenever the rooftop is used, and confirm after-hours access rules with the library. Equipment includes A/V, sponsor materials, and supplies.

    Date Selection¶

    Dates are chosen by conflict-checking a spreadsheet of Austin events: Easter, Passover, Texas Relays, Cap10k, the kite festival, SXSW proximity, graduations. The Chair runs this ~11 weeks before the current conference so next year's contract can be signed right after it. See next-year-planning.md.

    Contract Mechanics¶

    • Nonprofit composite hourly rate; the contract covers three 13-hour days (6:00 AM to 7:00 PM)
    • 50% deposit at signing, with a 14-day deadline to return the signed agreement
    • Balance due about 3 months before the event (Jan 10-11 for an April conference)
    • Cancellation tiers at 91+ days, 31-90 days, and 30 days
    • Insurance certificate and A/V-vendor coordination required; the library emails about this ~2 months out
    • File amendments (space or hours changes) as soon as space or hours change; do not sit on them
    • Library pricing may rise year over year; get the next-year contract signed by February 1

    Timeline¶

    12+ Months Out¶

    Sign next year's facility use agreement immediately after the current conference and pay the deposit.

    Selection criteria if considering a move: - Capacity for 250-275 (the current sell-out range; 350 theater max at the library) - Accessible (transit, parking) - A/V or vendor support - Food service options - Within budget (hotel routes have failed on room-block risk and F&B minimums) - Mid-April dates available

    6-8 Months Out¶

    • Confirm dates and spaces
    • Amendment if space or hours change
    • Insurance requirements
    • Load-in/out times
    • Venue restrictions (signage, food, decorations)

    3-4 Months Out¶

    • Tour with committee leads
    • Plan layouts and capacity
    • Reg desk location
    • Sponsor booth/table locations
    • Signage placement
    • Coordinate with A/V vendor

    1-2 Months Out¶

    • Final room setup confirmed
    • Vendor delivery schedules (catering, A/V, swag)
    • Attendee venue maps
    • Emergency procedures and exits
    • Day-of role assignments

    A/V Coordination¶

    See av.md. The library's complimentary base A/V (one wireless mic, one ceiling projector, lectern) is insufficient, so an outside A/V vendor is hired every year. Signal chain: speaker to A/V vendor to library system.

    Friday Thank-You Dinner¶

    Audience: speakers + sponsors (1-2/company) + Friends of PyTexas + organizers + key volunteers.

    Location options: 1. Rooftop Garden (if weather) 2. Local restaurant private room 3. Catered alternative venue

    Checklist: - [ ] Pick location - [ ] Headcount from Speaker + Finance - [ ] Invites 2-3 weeks out - [ ] Dietary restrictions - [ ] Catering or restaurant reservation - [ ] Seating that mixes groups - [ ] Welcome/thank-you remarks - [ ] Transportation if needed

    Coordinate budget with Finance.

    Catering¶

    See catering.md. Venue-specific: - Cold storage at library, no full kitchen - Bring catering from external vendors - The library provides no linens; rent them - Coordinate access times and delivery windows

    Schedule: - Saturday: breakfast/coffee 7:30-10, snacks mid-afternoon, lunch out 12:30-2 - Sunday: same as Saturday (adjust for typical attendance drop) - Friday night: appreciation dinner (above)

    Quotes gathered ~8-10 weeks out, deposits ~6.5 weeks out, final orders ~4.5 weeks out. Considerations: dietary handling, delivery windows, serving style, pricing, prior conference experience.

    Volunteers¶

    See volunteers.md for roles, shift planning, and recruitment; the counts there are guidance, not recorded staffing levels.

    Day-of staffing runs thin every year. Plan registration coverage plus a floater or two in 2-3 hour rotations, keep the shirt table staffed, and put A/V monitoring on shifts.

    Recruitment channels: marketing, local Python meetups, Discord, student orgs, personal asks (highest conversion). Incentives (shirt, free or comped ticket, early swag access) are listed in volunteers.md.

    A central volunteer coordinator (Venue lead or designate) owns the schedule; station leads (registration, A/V, sponsors) run their own briefings.

    Appreciation: - [ ] Volunteer shirts or designation - [ ] Friday dinner if capacity allows - [ ] Public thank-you opening/closing - [ ] Post-event thank-you email with photos

    Attendee Experience¶

    Signage¶

    Map signage on a Miro board (~6 standing signs, 5-10 single-page); check whether the library owns sign holders.

    • Room signs per track
    • Directional to reg, restrooms, sponsor area
    • Schedule boards or displays
    • Emergency exit signs (supplement venue)
    • Sponsor signage with Finance

    Maps¶

    Library map.

    • Print for registration packets
    • Digital version on site
    • Highlight: reg, talks, sponsors, restrooms, food

    Accessibility¶

    • ADA compliance verified
    • Accessible route to all areas
    • Front-row seating reserved for needs
    • Captioning via A/V vendor
    • Quiet/sensory break room
    • Accessibility needs collected at registration

    Networking Spaces¶

    • Hallway conversation spots
    • Informal seating
    • Laptop tables
    • Good flow between sponsor area and commons

    Day-Of Logistics¶

    Friday Morning (Load-In and Setup)¶

    There is no Thursday access; everything below happens Friday morning in parallel with tutorials.

    • Venue access at 6:00 AM; Conference Chair arrives at open
    • Photograph venue property at load-in (and again at load-out)
    • A/V vendor setup + testing
    • Reg desk setup (registration hours: Friday 9-12 and 2-5)
    • Signage
    • Sponsor booth setup
    • Swag delivery + organization
    • Coffee/breakfast setup
    • Costco + HEB supply run during the morning tutorial

    Team Coordination¶

    Day-of leads: registration, A/V + speaker liaison, sponsor liaison, volunteer coordinator, rooftop event, emergency point person.

    Comms: shared channel (Slack/Discord/walkies), phone list, venue direct line for emergencies.

    Emergency¶

    • All emergency exits identified
    • First aid kit location
    • Venue security/management contact
    • Nearest hospital
    • Evacuation announcement plan
    • Designated emergency liaison

    Load-Out + Cleanup (Sunday)¶

    • Pack registration materials
    • A/V vendor breakdown coordinated
    • Collect signage
    • Organize leftover swag for transport
    • Sponsor materials returned or disposed per preference
    • Trash removal
    • Final walkthrough with venue staff
    • Return keys / access badges

    Post-conference: - [ ] Venue thank-you - [ ] Document any issues for insurance - [ ] Final payment if outstanding - [ ] Gather feedback for next year

    Next-Year Venue Planning¶

    Watch for pricing changes each cycle; if pricing jumps, the Conference Chair gathers alternatives before recommitting. The standard booking is the event center only: no rooftop, no demo area.

    The annual rhythm: - Fall: scouting and quote gathering, target a signed contract by Feb 1 - ~11 weeks out: dates chosen via the Austin events conflict spreadsheet - ~9 weeks out: deposit paid

    Alternative criteria: capacity, central Austin, transit, A/V comparable, pricing, mid-April dates available.

    Venue Appreciation¶

    • Thank-you notes to key contacts
    • Small gift / token
    • Public recognition on site + social
    • Professional photos of their space
    • Offer testimonial / reference
    • Early commitment for next year if returning

    Cross-Committee¶

    • Finance: contract budget, demo space pricing, catering budget, insurance
    • Marketing: venue photos, maps, parking comms
    • Speaker: room capacities, A/V capability, green room
    • Community: streaming, WiFi for virtual, Discord coordinator space

    Resources¶

    • Library Map
    • The executed facility use agreement, amendments, and venue spec sheets live in the year's Drive folder under Venue

    Pitfalls¶

    • Hallway echo carries into session rooms; sound deadening is an open problem.
    • Ask the library for reserved organizer parking; reserving is routine.
    • Decide the hotel room block question early; it tends to drift unresolved.
